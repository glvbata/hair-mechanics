# Review Playbook — Hair Mechanics

**Why this is priority #1:** Hair Mechanics sits at **4.5★ / 83 reviews**. Google's
local pack weights review count, rating, velocity, and recency heavily. Competitors in
Auburn are typically 4.8★ with several hundred reviews. Until that gap closes, the
website can rank perfectly and still lose the click to the three businesses in the map
box above it.

**Target:** 4.7★ and 200+ reviews. At 8–10 new reviews/week that's roughly one quarter.

---

## The three asks

### 1. In-chair ask (highest conversion, do this every cut)

Right after the mirror check, while the client is still happy:

> "Glad you like it. If you've got 20 seconds — there's a QR on the counter for a
> Google review. Helps a ton with people finding us."

Then hand them the card or point at the mirror card. **Ask while they're in the chair,
not at the door.** Once they're standing they're gone.

Barbers should ask **every** satisfied client. Not the ones who seem enthusiastic —
everyone. Selective asking is what keeps shops stuck at 4.5.

### 2. QR card (passive backup)

Generated by `npm run review-card` → `docs/review-card.html`. Print it, cut it, place:

- One at the register
- One at **each** mirror station
- One taped inside the waiting area

QR points to `hairmechanics.net/review?src=qr`.

### 3. Follow-up text (catches the ones who said "sure" and forgot)

Send **the same evening**, 3–4 hours after the appointment. Not next day.

> Hey [name] — Glen at Hair Mechanics. Thanks for coming in today. If you've got a sec,
> a quick Google review really helps the shop: hairmechanics.net/review?src=sms

Rules:
- Only to clients who were actually happy
- Once. Never a second reminder
- Never to a bulk list of old clients — Google's filter strips sudden spikes from stale
  contacts, and it can look like review buying

## What to ask them to mention

The page at `/review` already nudges this, but reinforce it verbally. Reviews that name
the **service** and the **city** are what make you surface for `fade auburn wa`,
`beard trim near me`, `barberia cerca de mi`:

- Which barber cut their hair
- What they got — fade, beard trim, kids cut, line-up
- That they're in Auburn (or Kent/Federal Way if they drove in)

Never script the review for them. Suggest the topics, not the words.

---

## Replying to reviews

**Reply to every single one, within 48 hours.** Replies are indexed text — this is free
keyword real estate, and it's a documented local ranking signal. It's also what AI
assistants read when summarizing the business.

**5-star:**
> Appreciate it, [name]! Glad the [fade / beard trim] came out right. See you next time
> here in Auburn. — Glen

**4-star:**
> Thanks for the honest feedback, [name]. Tell us what would've made it a 5 next time —
> we'll get it dialed in. — Glen

**1–3 star:**
> Sorry we missed the mark, [name]. That's not our standard. Give me a call at
> (206) 399-9288 and I'll make it right. — Glen

Never argue publicly. One reply, take it offline, move on. A calm reply to a bad review
converts readers better than the bad review costs you.

---

## Hard rules (Google policy — violating these can wipe the whole profile)

- **No incentives.** No free line-up, no discount, no raffle entry for a review. This is
  an explicit Google Terms violation and gets reviews purged.
- **No gating.** Don't screen for happy clients first and route only them to Google.
  Asking everyone who's satisfied is fine; building a filter that intercepts unhappy
  people is a violation.
- **No shop devices.** Don't let clients leave reviews on the shop's iPad or wifi.
  Multiple reviews from one IP/device get filtered as fake.
- **No bulk blasts** to old client lists.

## Measurement

`/review` fires a GA4 `review_click` event with a `source` param (`qr`, `sms`, or
`direct`). That closes the loop that was previously invisible:

| What you'll see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Low `review_click` count | Barbers aren't asking. Coaching problem. |
| High clicks, flat review count | People bounce at the Google form. Check the link on a real phone. |
| `qr` ≫ `sms` | Cards work, texts aren't going out. |
| `sms` ≫ `qr` | Cards aren't placed where people look. |

Check weekly alongside `npm run ga4:pull`. Update `RATING` and `REVIEWS_COUNT` in
`src/constants/business.ts` monthly — they feed the homepage, every area page, and the
schema.

# GBP Post Drafts — 4 weeks

Ready to paste into Google Business Profile. Each post lives ~7 days in the
carousel before archiving, so weekly cadence is the sweet spot.

**How to post:** GBP dashboard → Promote → "Add update" → paste title + body
→ pick CTA → upload photo → publish. Takes ~90 seconds per post.

**Why these specifically:** each one targets a real query cluster from our GSC
+ GBP data, with copy that doubles as a ranking signal (the GBP listing's
description text feeds into the local-pack algorithm).

## After week 4 — what to do

Three options once these run their course:

1. **Repeat the cycle** — re-post variants of the same 4 themes. GBP posts archive after 7 days; same theme on a new post is fine and Google rewards consistent posting.
2. **Switch to data-driven** — by mid-May we'll have a 2nd GSC report. Run `npm run seo:diff` and let the queries that *moved up* drive the next month's posts.
3. **Add seasonal hooks** — Memorial Day weekend hours, Father's Day combo deal, back-to-school cuts in late August. Tactical, not strategic, but they convert.

My call: option 2. Let real movement guide what gets promoted next.

---

## What this won't do

GBP posts are a **signal**, not a magic switch. They:
- ✅ Boost map-pack ranking modestly (Google sees the listing as actively maintained)
- ✅ Add a CTA touchpoint for visitors who land on the GBP card from Maps
- ✅ Reinforce keywords in the listing's text profile

They don't:
- ❌ Move organic web rankings directly (that's the on-site work we already shipped)
- ❌ Trigger notifications to existing customers
- ❌ Build links

Treat them as a 5-min/week habit, not a campaign.

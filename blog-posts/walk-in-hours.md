# Does Hair Mechanics Take Walk-Ins? (+ Best Times to Come In Auburn, WA)

You need a fresh cut but you didn't book ahead — it happens. Whether you're squeezing in a trim before an event or just passing by on A Street, you're probably wondering: *can I walk into Hair Mechanics without an appointment?*

Short answer: yes. Here's what you need to know.

## Yes, We Take Walk-Ins at Hair Mechanics

Hair Mechanics is a walk-in friendly barbershop. You don't need to book in advance to get a haircut — just stop by at **1251 A Street NE, Auburn, WA 98002** and we'll get you taken care of.

That said, like any busy barbershop, wait times can vary depending on the day and time. If you want to minimize your wait, a little timing goes a long way.

## Best Times to Walk In (Lower Wait Times)

If you want to walk in and get seated quickly, here are the windows that tend to move faster:

**Weekday mornings (Monday–Friday, 10am–12pm)**
Right when we open during the week is typically our calmest time. Most people are at work, which means you can often walk in and get straight into the chair. This is the sweet spot for a walk-in haircut in Auburn with little to no wait.

**Mid-week afternoons**
Tuesday through Thursday in the early afternoon tends to be more relaxed than Friday. If your schedule is flexible, Wednesday or Thursday around 1–3pm is a solid window.

**What to Avoid**
Friday afternoons and Saturdays are our busiest stretches — especially between noon and 5pm. Walk-ins are still welcome, but expect a longer wait during peak weekend hours. Sunday mornings right at 8am can also fill up fast as people prep for the week ahead.

## Our Hours

We're open seven days a week:

- **Monday–Friday:** 10am – 8pm
- **Saturday–Sunday:** 8am – 8pm

Weekend hours starting at 8am give early risers a real advantage. If you're up and need a fresh cut before your Saturday plans, getting here early is the move.

# How Much Does a Haircut Cost at Hair Mechanics in Auburn, WA?

One of the most common questions we get before a first visit: *how much is a haircut at Hair Mechanics?* It's a fair question — nobody wants to show up and be surprised at the register. So here's a straightforward breakdown of what you can expect to pay at our Auburn barbershop.

## What Affects the Price of a Haircut?

Barber pricing varies based on a few things: the type of service, the complexity of the cut, and the shop itself. A basic trim is going to cost less than a full fade with a hard part and beard shaping — that's just the nature of the work.

At Hair Mechanics, we price our services based on what's actually involved. Here's a general look at our service categories:

**Haircuts and Fades**
A standard adult haircut — scissors or clipper work, styled and finished — starts at a competitive rate for the Auburn area. Fade haircuts, which require more precision and blending technique, are priced accordingly. Whether you're getting a low, mid, or high fade, you're paying for a barber who knows how to make the gradients look seamless.

**Beard Services**
Beard trims and beard shaping are separate services, though we can bundle them with a haircut. A beard trim is a cleanup and line-up. Beard shaping involves more sculptural work — defining your neckline, cheeklines, and overall shape. Both are available on their own or as an add-on to your cut.

**Kids Cuts**
We offer haircuts for kids too. Pricing for kids' cuts is typically lower than adult cuts, making Hair Mechanics a good spot for the whole family to come in on a Saturday morning.

## How Do Hair Mechanics Prices Compare in Auburn?

For the South King County area, our pricing is fair and reflects the quality of work you're getting. Auburn has a range of places to get a haircut — chain salons, discount shops, and independent barbershops. What you get at Hair Mechanics is experienced barbers (Glen, Angel, and Michael all take pride in their work), a clean shop, and cuts that hold up between visits.

If you've been burned by a cheap cut before, you know the real cost isn't the price tag — it's having to live with a bad fade for the next three weeks.
